Our Journey

Our Beginnings, Your Future.

Every business begins with a dream of an idea, but it takes structure and time to make it something meaningful by planning, being creative and a clear understanding for the target audience. Our journey always started with a start and end story, by having a interest in technology and the role devices play in modern life. iPlug was created around the idea of making technology more accessible to consumers, as well as providing communication and entertainment to work, technology has become an essential part of how people manage their everyday activities.

This all started in a small room, with just a mindmap to discuss how this present idea would grow into a successful business. Born and raised in England, London, the warehouse currently that distributes your shopping is based in North London, where we are seeking to deliver your product with high satisfaction and remark.

We recognise that we need to pave a way for customers to discover items that suit their lifestyles, interests and budgets. This provided the inspiration to start up iPlug, to develop an online technology store where customers can browse electronic products and accessories in one handy place.

iPlug's starting point was recognising just how closely technology is connected to everyday life. No matter the age group, this is a target audience for everyone as they are no longer limited to specialist users and rather used in everyday life by students, professionals, families, content creators and fitness-minded people.

Products such as headphones, smartwatches, phone accessories and other electronic essentials serve different purposes. In the end, they all form part of the way people interact with technology. We saw an opportunity to create a business around this demand by bringing relevant products together through an online shopping platform. We are more than happy to see where this has brought us and we hope to expand and strengthen our products alongside increasing our satisfaction never than ever.
